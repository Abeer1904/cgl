About the game

Read the
climate signals.

Climate Crusaders is a vocabulary-building board game designed to give communities, students, and practitioners the language to name, discuss, and act on climate change. Players identify climate phenomena, causes, and responses through a rapid riddle-and-recognition structure — building fluency through play rather than lecture.

The game is built around a practical insight: you cannot respond to what you cannot name. Building shared climate vocabulary within communities and institutions is a prerequisite for meaningful participation in adaptation and mitigation decisions. Climate Crusaders makes that vocabulary accessible without requiring prior expertise.

01 — Design

Climate literacy
co-design

Built through engagement with climate practitioners, community educators, and frontline adaptation workers across India. Design workshops mapped the vocabulary gap — the specific terms and concepts that were blocking participation in climate-relevant decisions — and shaped the game around them.

The design question: what is the minimum climate vocabulary that enables meaningful participation in adaptation planning? The answer produced a curated set of 60+ terms and phenomena that form the game's content base.

02 — Development

Gameplay &
mechanics

A board game for 2–6 players. 45–90 minutes. Suitable for ages 12 and above.

Board & cards

Players move across a board representing a climate landscape — identifying phenomena, causes, and responses through riddle cards and visual recognition.

Riddle structure

Each card presents a riddle describing a climate phenomenon or response. Players identify the term, discuss its meaning, and place their marker.

Community variant

A community facilitation variant allows larger groups to play collaboratively — surfacing shared knowledge and identifying vocabulary gaps for follow-up discussion.

Format

Board game for 2–6 players. 45–90 minutes. Designed for schools, communities, and organisation-level climate awareness programmes.

03 — Deployment

How it runs
in the field

Climate Crusaders is used in schools, community programmes, and climate literacy initiatives across India.

School programmes

Used in school climate education programmes as an engaging alternative to lecture-format climate curricula.

Community settings

Deployed in village and community-level climate adaptation programmes to build shared vocabulary before participatory planning.

Organisation training

Run in staff orientation and capacity building programmes for climate-relevant NGOs, CSOs, and government departments.

Outcomes

What changes

Participants develop a working climate vocabulary — the language to name, discuss, and act on climate change.

Climate vocabulary

60+ climate terms become familiar through play — accessible to participants with no prior climate education.

Phenomena recognition

Players develop the ability to recognise and name climate signals in their own environment — a foundational adaptation capacity.

Community language

Groups emerge with shared vocabulary for climate discussions — enabling more meaningful participation in planning processes.

Awareness foundation

The game creates a knowledge foundation for deeper engagement with adaptation planning, mitigation action, and climate advocacy.
