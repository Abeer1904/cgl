About the game

Map the
climate pathways.

Climate Quest is a pathway-building card game for understanding the chains of cause, effect, and response in climate change. Players match climate drivers to impacts, and impacts to adaptation or mitigation actions — building a working map of climate pathways at community and institutional scale.

Where Climate Crusaders builds vocabulary, Climate Quest builds systems understanding — the ability to trace climate cause-effect chains and identify where intervention is possible. It is designed for practitioners, planners, and communities who need to move from vocabulary to action.

01 — Design

Climate systems
research

Built through engagement with climate scientists, adaptation planners, district-level climate officials, and community practitioners. The pathway structure was designed to capture the actual cause-effect logic of climate impacts — not a simplified version, but an accurate one made accessible through game mechanics.

The design question: how do you make climate systems thinking accessible to practitioners who were not trained as climate scientists? The answer was pathway cards — discrete, combinable, accurate building blocks that reveal system logic through assembly.

02 — Development

Gameplay &
mechanics

A card game for 2–8 players. 60–120 minutes. Designed for practitioners, planners, and communities.

Pathway cards

Three card types: Driver cards (emissions, land use, deforestation), Impact cards (flood, drought, heat, migration), and Action cards (adaptation, mitigation, resilience).

Chain building

Players build cause-effect chains by connecting Driver → Impact → Action. Valid chains score points; invalid connections trigger discussion.

Scenario mode

A facilitated scenario mode presents a specific climate context — a district under flood stress, a farming community facing drought — and asks players to map the full pathway and design responses.

Format

Card game for 2–8 players. 60–120 minutes. Designed for climate training, district planning workshops, and community adaptation programmes.

03 — Deployment

How it runs
in the field

Climate Quest is deployed in climate training, district planning, and community adaptation contexts.

District planning

Used in district climate action planning workshops to build shared understanding of climate pathways before planning decisions are made.

Climate training

Deployed in government, NGO, and donor organisation climate capacity building as an experiential systems-thinking module.

Community programmes

Run in climate-vulnerable community programmes to build local capacity for pathway analysis and adaptation planning.

Outcomes

What changes

Participants develop climate systems understanding — the capacity to trace pathways and identify intervention points.

Pathway fluency

Players develop the ability to map climate cause-effect chains — a practical capacity for adaptation planning and climate communication.

Action literacy

Understanding the difference between adaptation, mitigation, and resilience actions enables more precise programme design.

Systems thinking

The pathway structure builds systems-level climate thinking — moving participants beyond single-variable explanations.

Planning foundation

Groups emerge with a shared climate systems map that can anchor district adaptation planning and institutional climate action.
