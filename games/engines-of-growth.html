About the game

Building
inside constraints.

Engines of Growth is a strategy simulation for understanding how innovation ecosystems develop — and why they often don't. Players take on roles as entrepreneurs, investors, government regulators, and institutional actors, navigating the political economy of growth: access to capital, regulatory friction, market failure, and the governance decisions that shape what is possible.

The simulation challenges a common assumption — that growth is primarily a matter of better entrepreneurs or better businesses. It reveals the institutional infrastructure that enables or blocks innovation, and what governance reforms would need to happen for an economy to become genuinely dynamic.

01 — Design

Innovation
ecosystem research

Built through engagement with startup ecosystem practitioners, economic policy researchers, industrial cluster developers, and regulatory reform specialists. Scenarios draw on real cases of innovation ecosystem development and failure across India and the Global South.

The design question: how do you create a playable environment where participants experience the systemic constraints on growth — not as an abstract policy framework, but as a set of decisions with real consequences?

02 — Development

Gameplay &
mechanics

A strategy simulation structured around a multi-round economic development challenge.

Roles

Entrepreneurs, institutional investors, government regulators, development finance actors, and community stakeholders — each with distinct capital, mandate, and time horizons.

Resource dynamics

Capital, talent, infrastructure, and regulatory clearance are scarce. Allocation decisions shape which ventures grow, which fail, and what economic ecosystem emerges.

Policy rounds

Government actors make regulatory and investment decisions that create or destroy conditions for innovation — with unintended consequences that emerge over simulation rounds.

Format

Facilitated simulation for 15–30 participants. 3–4 hours. Designed for economic policy training, enterprise development programmes, and governance reform contexts.

03 — Deployment

How it runs
in the field

Engines of Growth is deployed in economic governance training, enterprise development, and innovation policy contexts.

Economic policy training

Used in civil service and regulatory training to build intuition for how governance decisions shape economic outcomes.

Enterprise programmes

Run in entrepreneurship and enterprise development contexts as a structured exploration of the ecosystem constraints on business growth.

Policy design

Deployed at the outset of economic reform design processes to create shared understanding of systemic constraints among stakeholders.

Outcomes

What changes

Participants build economic governance literacy — how systems enable or block growth, and what can change.

Economic vocabulary

Market failure, regulatory friction, capital access, ecosystem infrastructure — terms become analytical tools.

Constraint empathy

Playing different roles builds intuition for why innovation is blocked in some contexts and enabled in others.

Policy design skill

Participants practice designing economic policies that account for incentive structures, second-order effects, and unintended consequences.

Systems thinking

Groups develop the habit of seeing economic development as a systemic challenge — not just an entrepreneurship challenge.
