About the game

The water
crisis is here.

It's in the Water combines narrative storytelling with system simulation to explore a community water crisis — contamination, denial, governance failure, and collective response. Players follow a story while making decisions that shape the crisis: who speaks up, who stays silent, what institutions respond, and whether the community organises.

The game reveals the governance and behavioural logic underneath water crises: why contamination goes unreported, why institutional response is delayed, and what combination of community action and institutional accountability enables resolution. It is designed for community health, WASH, and civic education contexts.

01 — Design

Community
water research

Built from research into community water crises across India — documented cases of groundwater contamination, piped water failure, and the gap between institutional mandate and community reality. Design workshops were run with community health workers, WASH practitioners, and water governance specialists.

The design question: how do you create a game that takes water seriously as both a governance challenge and a community health challenge? The story-simulation hybrid format emerged as the answer — combining the emotional engagement of narrative with the analytical depth of simulation.

02 — Development

Gameplay &
mechanics

A story-simulation hybrid for groups of 10–20 participants. 2–3 hours.

Narrative structure

Players follow a community water story across three stages: contamination and denial, escalation and response, resolution or failure. The story is real enough to feel consequential.

Decision points

At key narrative moments, players make decisions — report, stay silent, organise, appeal to authority — with consequences that shape the story's direction.

System reveal

As the story progresses, the governance and incentive systems beneath the crisis become visible — turning emotional engagement into analytical understanding.

Format

Story-simulation for 10–20 participants. 2–3 hours. Designed for community health, WASH, and civic education programmes.

03 — Deployment

How it runs
in the field

It's in the Water is deployed in community health, WASH, and civic education programmes across India.

Community health

Used in community health programmes to build water safety knowledge and civic response capacity at the community level.

WASH programmes

Deployed in water, sanitation, and hygiene programmes as an experiential engagement with community water governance.

Civic education

Run in civic education and community mobilisation contexts as a narrative anchor for discussions of rights, accountability, and collective action.

Outcomes

What changes

Participants build water governance literacy and community health knowledge through a story that makes both feel personal.

Water safety knowledge

Participants develop practical knowledge of water contamination, health risks, and access to safe water — grounded in community contexts.

Governance literacy

The simulation reveals how water governance works — and why it often fails — building civic knowledge about accountability and rights.

Civic agency

Players experience how community action enables crisis response — building the confidence and vocabulary for civic engagement.

Systems empathy

The story structure builds empathy for the multiple actors in a water crisis — understanding why silence persists and what enables speaking up.
