The work

Learning by rehearsing real choices.

Health programmes often ask people to remember guidance under pressure. Our games give communities, facilitators, and frontline workers a way to rehearse choices before those choices become urgent.

Nutrition
Poshan ki Potli

Dietary diversity, food groups, and local recipes as a practical card-based learning system.
Early nutrition
Purak Ahaar

Complementary feeding choices and early-life food practices translated into a playable format.
Frontline response
Chuppi Todo

Domestic violence literacy, referral pathways, and institutional support rehearsed safely.
