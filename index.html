How we work
01 — Design
Translate.

We translate complex public problems into playable systems — governance challenges, climate futures, digital transitions, public health crises — broken down into roles, constraints, resources, and consequences. Every game begins with field research, systems mapping, and co-creation with the communities and institutions that live inside the problem.

Governance Climate Futures Digital Transitions Public Health Roles Constraints Resources Consequences
02 — Development
Play.

Then we play. Participants navigate real decisions, surface trade-offs, and see consequences before those consequences become irreversible. This is not simulation as metaphor. It is simulation as method — built from twenty games, ninety-five causal rules, and sixteen mechanics extracted from eight years of field-tested design.

03 — Deployment
Embed.

Then we embed. The goal is not a single event. It is a practice that persists inside the institution — a replicable system that can be applied across governance challenges, scaled across contexts, and adapted without starting from scratch.
