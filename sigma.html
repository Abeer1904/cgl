What SIGMA
publishes.

SIGMA is a space for speculative storytelling and thinking — and for the people, practices, and questions that refuse easy categorisation.

It sits at the intersection of civic imagination and cultural practice, publishing essays, interviews, creative work, and critical reflection from across South Asia and beyond.

Storytelling

Narratives as civic context, voice, empathy, and social action. Stories that situate communities inside the systems they live in.

Imagination

Essays that treat imagination as a public capability, not a private luxury. Speculation as civic method.

Games

Analogue play, systems thinking, learning, and citizenship through interaction. Games as more than entertainment.

Multimedia & Arts

Comics, performance, installations, and hybrid forms as civic method. Practice that refuses to be disciplined.

From the editor

Why this
journal.

SIGMA exists because the questions it asks don't belong to any single discipline. They belong to practitioners — people who make things, who run programmes, who teach in classrooms, who design games and write stories and build civic spaces.

This is a journal for them. And for the thinkers who want to be in conversation with practice, not only with theory.
